## Job 1 — the frame (the owner's first complaint)
Goal, measured on the same 3 views as RP1B (castle gate, open fields, outer mill), `renderer.info` of the main pass, hero mode, high preset: **≤ 150 calls and ≤ 400 k triangles**, programs +0. All **only in hero mode** (gate off = byte-identical behaviour; `exit()` restores everything). **Without editing world files**, by working on the scene graph at hero entry and per frame:
1. `cull.js` — **spatial chunking of repeated geometry:** every `InstancedMesh` above N instances (trees, rocks, worksite life packs, grass…) is split at hero entry into grid cells (≈ 48 m) kept as lightweight sub-ranges (e.g. sorted instances + `count`/`setDrawRange`-style per-cell draws, or sub-InstancedMeshes sharing geometry and material), then drawn only if the cell is inside the draw radius (`REACH.k`) and the frustum. The originals are hidden and restored on exit. Anything the game animates per instance every frame (`instanceMatrix` rewritten by the world) must keep working — find those (grep `instanceMatrix.needsUpdate`) and either re-sync cheaply or leave them whole and list them.
2. **Shadow casters:** the hero's shadow box covers ≈ 60–80 m around her; casters outside it (map-wide packs) are removed from the shadow pass (`castShadow=false` at entry, restored on exit); report the shadow-pass call count before/after.
3. **Static merge:** castle/town meshes that never move (walls, towers, houses) sharing one material → merged per cell at hero entry (`BufferGeometryUtils.mergeGeometries`) — only if it does not break picking/`world.picks`, destruction or tier swaps (TR2 will change these meshes at level-ups: re-merge on the world's rebuild event, or skip merging for anything the world can rebuild and report that). If it cannot be done safely without world-file hooks, say so and do 1+2 only.
4. **Honest numbers:** a CPU-throttled run (Chromium `Emulation.setCPUThrottlingRate` 4× and 6×, software GL for GPU cost aside) gives JS ms per frame; report it but say it is not a phone. The governor stays as the safety net. Ask the owner for ONE thing at the end: the `perf` lines from his server (`journalctl -u bluebot-wall --since "30 min ago" | grep perf | tail -40`) after he plays on the phone.
