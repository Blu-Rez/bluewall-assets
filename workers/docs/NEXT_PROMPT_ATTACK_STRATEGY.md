### 3.3 Reference (CoC / Siege-style facts to borrow; sources: guildorder CoC strategy page, gamezebo Siegefall guide, general CoC knowledge)

## 4. Design — "Strategy Layer v1" (data-driven; budget-neutral)
**Principles:** P1 counters, not stats (each unit/defence has ≥1 good and ≥1 bad matchup). P2 what you see = what it does (cannon-looking site IS a cannon). P3 information before commitment (≤2 taps to read a base). P4 real decisions: first target, squad split/timing, spell spot, core-vs-outskirts time split. P5 **budget-neutral**: total base DPS/HP `D` stays (R7 calibration holds) — rules *redistribute*. P6 phone-cheap.

### 4.1 Five axes
1. **Layer** — attackers: air = pegasus, baby, dragon, dragonling (ignore walls/water/collision, can be sent to any point incl. inside the walls, can't be hit by melee guards or ground-only defences); **wading ground** = giant, hill giant (walk through water/moat, still blocked by walls); everything else ground (water only via bridges). Defenders: melee guards ground-only; archers/wall archers both.
2. **Damage class × size class** (the rock-paper-scissors core). Size from `space`: light ≤2, medium 3–6, heavy 7–12, huge ≥16 (+air flag). Starting multipliers (tune with the harness):

| defence (sites on map) | hits | behaviour | light | medium | heavy/huge | air |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Arrow tower (15) | both | rapid single, nearest | 1.5 | 1.0 | 0.6 | 1.0 |
| **Cannon** (6 = every 2nd weapon site, as the visuals already alternate) | **ground only** | splash 7 m on the densest ground group; **min range 14 m = blind spot**; slow | 1.6 | 1.0 | 0.5 | — |
| **Ballista** (6) | both | **air first**, then heavies; single target (no splash) | 0.5 | 1.0 | 2.2 | 1.3 |
| Keep | both | last stand, nearest | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| Tesla (premium, ≤4) | both | chain 3 (as is) | 1.3 | 1.0 | 0.8 | 1.0 |
| Guards (melee) | ground | body-block, kill raiders/siege | — | — | — | can't hit |
Weights per site stay as today (`wOf`: tower 1, ballista/cannon 2.2, keep 1.2, tesla 2) so total DPS is unchanged. Resulting triangle: **swarm/light** beats single-target (arrow/ballista waste shots) but dies to cannon splash; **heavy/tank** beats arrow/cannon but dies to ballista; **air** beats cannons/walls but dies to ballista/arrow focus; **siege** outranges (trebuchet 124 > ballista 92) but dies to raiders/guards.
3. **Role + favourite target** (data field `prefer`, per unit): giant/hill/guard/shieldmaiden = defences (draw fire); **ram = gate/wall breaker** (×4 vs wall/gate, ignores units, vulnerable to guards/cannon splash); catapult/cannon/trebuchet = stand off outside enemy range, defences first; **raiders** (cavalry, werewolf, imp, pegasus) = outlying non-defence buildings outside coverage (this is how 100 % is reached in time); archer/mage = nearest, keep distance; baby/dragon = anti-air sites first. Ground units pick the cheapest breach (open gate > weakest wall); walls are chosen on purpose when that is the cheapest path.
4. **Geometry** — build a **threat grid** (8–10 m cells, per layer: Σ DPS of defences that can hit that layer there, cannon min-range hole included) once per battle. Uses: scout overlay, AI scoring (raiders avoid, siege keeps out), result analysis. **No entry-choice UI** (owner 16:52: he already sends every squad wherever he wants; the muster stays in front of `gates[0]`). Keep the cost honest instead: muster (≈169,85) → far outskirts is 300–410 m ≈ 35–46 s for infantry, 65–92 s for siege (speed = `spd`×9 m/s), ≈ 20 s for cavalry — that walk is a real part of the 180 s plan; never shorten it by magic. (Only if he asks later: start side east/west via `meta.gates[1]`.) **Base variety on a shared map:** bots get a seeded *profile* (AA-heavy / artillery-heavy / balanced / arrow-only; which sites are cannon vs ballista, active sites by level as in plan C §2.9), players get it deterministically from their levels; later (owner OK only) let him choose the weapon per site at home. Be honest with him: the map shape is still shared.
5. **Time/objective** — show each building's % of destruction (defences ≈58 %, outskirts, houses, walls); 3-minute cap forces a split between "kill the guns" and "sweep the outskirts". Make the middle of the battle matter: guard posts **react** (sally to intercept raiders in the outskirts), stronger posts at barracks/lair/vaults (no new meshes — they are people).
