## Texture memory — the finding that changes the plan
GPU estimate per texture: **1K = 5.33 MB, 512 = 1.33 MB, 256 = 0.33 MB.** At 1K the castle alone is 27 MB (5 kinds, one tier) / 53 MB (half/half), and the ~70 house / civic / production models of the home map would be ≈ 370 MB — far over the 120 MB budget. But at the game's closest zoom (7.7 px/m, 15 px/m at pixel ratio 2) a 14 m wall is 108 / 216 px wide: a 1K texture holds 5–9× more detail than the screen shows. `sheet_TR2_S2_texsize_phone_7px_per_m.png` / `…15px_per_m.png` (wall, tower, gate, cottage, hall at 1K / 512 / 256, looked at): **512 is indistinguishable at 7.7 px/m and clean at 15 px/m; 256 only softens the big landmark facades at 15 px/m.**
**Proposed (decide by number; default applies):** ship **512** for castle / houses / civic / production / defence / traps / bridges / animals / people, **1K** only for the keep + civic hall + market hall, **256** for nature packs, lamps and small props. Castle half/half: 53 → **13 MB**; files ≈ half the size (155 vs 300 KB per house). `tools/tr2_retex.mjs IN OUT 512` does it at ship time (geometry untouched). I will apply it as each stage ships its models.
