## 2. QA1 camera list (006 007 008 009 010 016 017 042 063) + 051
- **Cause:** `placeCamera` took `max(0.55, t·D − 0.25)`: any wall, pole, tent or house behind her squeezed the camera to 0.55 m = the back of her cloak / hair (BEFORE frames).
- **Fix (rpg.js `placeCamera0`):** the camera keeps **CAM_MIN = 3.0 m (3-D)** from her. If less room is left behind, it **rises over her and looks down at her** (up at once, down slowly, `CAM_RISE` 3.2 m); the raised point is re-tested against boxes (walls / roofs) and the ground; the floor 0.55 m is now 0.15 m (the old floor put the camera *inside* a wall nearer than that); where even that cannot get away (a roof right above) her head is hidden below 1.2 m like in first person (never the back of her hair). Poles / trees (infinite 2-D discs) and the ≤ 2 m mesh grid no longer stop a *raised* camera (`segHit(..., hi)`). Only third person, only the castle map, only on the ground (wall-walk / climb / underworld / first person unchanged). Dev: `__rp.camFix(false|true)`, `__rp.camInfo()`.
- **Numbers** (`rp1c4_cam_scan.js`: 10 488 spots = 12 m grid × 4 yaws over the walkable map, real `update()` + `placeCamera()`, 0.25 s each): camera < 2.15 m from her: **OFF 367 (3.5 %, min 0.58 m) → ON 3 (0.03 %, min 0.69 m, head hidden)**. Inside a solid (`camCheck`): OFF 28 → ON 26 — the 26 are 4 yaws at ~6 spots INSIDE the gate arches (the wall box with the carved arch: by design).
- **Frames:** `RP1C4_cam_{a..e}_BEFORE/AFTER.png` (844×390, full size) + `RP1C4_cam_sheet_after_3m.png`. **The QA1 stand-spot coordinates are not in the docs**, so I could not reproduce S04 / S15 / S20 / S25 / S32 by name; a–e are the five worst clusters of the scan (a −150,−54 · b 6,54 · c −306,−126 · d 54,90 · e 102,−6). Same defect class (the camera at 0.55 m).
- **051 (bridges):** `rp1c4_bridge_push.js`: **15** bridges (not 13) × 3 places × both sides, 3 s sideways push with the real `update()`: 43 stay on the deck, 26 fall in and swim by the wade rule (`sw = 2`), 21 end on the bank; **0 hovering** (grounded > 0.35 m above the ground while not swimming). No change was needed in the move code. The parapet colliders (so she cannot walk off at all) are TR2's — not done. **052** (inner wall face texture) = TR2 / GR1, **not touched — mother please pass on**. **063** (50° bank at bridge 8, streaked slope) = terrain / texture (GR1), not touched.
