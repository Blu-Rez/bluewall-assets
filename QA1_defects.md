## NOT proven / limits of this inspection (be honest)

- **Phone feel is not proven.** Every frame is software GL (swiftshader, 2 CPU cores), quality `high`, 844x390, dpr 1. No GPU, no touch, no real frame time; anything that depends on GPU precision (z-fight distance, shadow acne, bloom, water reflection tiles) may look different on a phone, better or worse.
- Day / dusk / night are the pinned hours 12.5 / 18.3 / 23 (one page load per hour; the clock is advanced by the tool, not by the game loop); transitions between hours are not looked at. Stations 25-45 (the extra bridges, edges, mill, worksites, field 2, inner wall) were taken at DAY only.
- Automatic checks cover a box of 70 m around each station (scan) and a 3 cm camera shift (z-fight probe at 422x195). A z-fight that only shows at 100 m+ or only while moving fast is NOT covered. Thin objects (masts) alias on a phone more than here. All numbers in this list come from tool page v4 or later (the first runs with a frozen LOD clock were thrown away and re-taken).
- The hero was placed by the tool on the nearest free dry spot (<= 40 m from the planned spot). Walking from station to station with the real physics was done in the dusk pass only (24 legs, 21 passed, 3 stalls, all three explained or retracted above); the 13 bridges were then walked in both directions and the 4 failing crossings were traced second by second. Reachability on foot of the other stations is not proven.
- The A lines about the bridges are what the game own proof walker (rp.walkTo: BFS on 1 m cells, then real physics) and free() / ground() say. They do NOT prove that a human could not find a way out (swim along the river to a gentle bank, jump, a respawn button); they prove that the straight way across fails at the listed bridges and that no side collision exists.
- No stairs / ramps / steps exist in the scene (CLIMB_ON=false), so the wall walk and tower tops are NOT inspected at all; the "stairs" and "wall walk" stations show the tower base by the gate only.
- Ugliness findings are my judgement of single frames at 844x390; items marked "doubtful" are on the list on purpose (you asked to be strict). Two objects stay unnamed (the black bar on the hill behind field 1 at S19, the black long hull in the north-edge camp at S40): the scene scan did not find them.
- Other quality presets (ultra / medium / low) and other weather (`w=clear` only) are not inspected. Crowds, fights and the UI layer were not part of this job.
