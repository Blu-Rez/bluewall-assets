## E1 open questions — closed with defaults (list)

From the E1 report (≤ 5, each had a default):
1. Resources add a small second price to upgrades (30 % of one building's output over the build time), gem prices unchanged → **yes**.
2. Producers rise with the castle level automatically (no builder, no separate upgrade); a new producer uses the 2 town crews → **automatic**.
3. Gems may top up a missing resource → **no** (not needed for pacing).
4. Tax default 10 %, max 30 %, −3 loyalty per 1 % tax, producers slow below loyalty 50 down to half → **as written** (the 30 % rate pays only from castle ~25).
5. Raids also take resources + 25 % of uncollected tax; shield by stars 4 / 8 / 12 h; attacking drops your own shield → **yes**.

Decided by EC1 (everything the E1 report left in "Not proven / not designed", plus the new 31–50 choices):
6. Tail pace of 31–50 → **medium**: the same ~29–30 days per castle level as 21–30 (flat `T_HOURS = 155`, `COST_K = 0.987`); short/long = change two numbers and rerun `ec1_test.py`.
7. E1 counts / outputs / stores for castle 31–50 → **the same formulas** as 1–30 (level factor 1.13, count steps, stores = 48 h), no new rules.
8. Army unit prices above 30 → **unchanged**; the ruby sink is repaired in the upgrade prices (`RUBY_TAIL`), because changing a unit price would change the 1–30 balance and the army catalogue the client shows.
9. Does the resource kind enter the round rule / `STRUCTS` → **no** (resources stay out of `STRUCTS`, as E1 said).
10. Boosts at 31–50 → **unchanged** (flat price per day, build/army/mine/royal); loot is not boosted; no new boost kinds.
11. Invites (1000 turquoise, max 50) and cup/league prizes stay **level-independent** (they are small against a round price of 3 M turquoise at castle 50; harmless).
12. Odd/even sawtooth → **kept** (it is the owner's own rhythm to castle 10 and the round rule).
13. Existing kingdoms when the resource layer goes live → **starter kit**: producers appear at the castle's level, every store is filled with 24 h of that level's output, nothing is taken away; paid for by nobody (design default, not implemented).
14. A forgotten producer (e.g. no quarry at castle 2) → **UI hint** on the blocked upgrade ("needs stone: build a quarry"), not an automatic gift.
