# TP1 — پرامپت‌های GPT برای عکس‌های ورودی Tripo (ملیکا: جلو / پشت / بغل)

**ترتیب:** اول «جلو» را با برگه‌ی طراحی ملیکا (`art:cast/melika.png`) بده. بعد «پشت» و «بغل» را با **برگه + عکس جلوی تأییدشده** بده تا صورت و لباس و مو یکی بمانند. خروجی‌ها را به همین ترتیب به Tripo بده: جلو، پشت، (بغل = left).
نسخه‌ی «بازی»: رب تا بالای مچ (رب بلند برگه موقع دویدن/لگد کشیده می‌شود). اگر نسخه‌ی دقیقِ برگه را خواستی، جمله‌ی «Costume for a game model…» را با «Keep the long robe exactly as in the sheet» عوض کن.

Output ONE photorealistic full-body picture of Melika for a 3D scan: FRONT view, facing the camera. A-pose: arms straight, angled about 45 degrees away from the body, hands open and empty with relaxed slightly spread fingers and palms toward the thighs; feet shoulder-width apart and flat on the floor; neutral calm face, mouth closed, eyes open looking at the camera. Camera straight at chest height, no perspective tilt, the whole body from hair to boots visible with a small margin, centred. Costume for a game model: green layered robe ending just above the ankles (no train, not touching the floor, a little narrower so the boots show), brown hooded cloak ending at the hips and hanging close to the body, dark brown curly hair with small white flowers, brown leather herb belt with pouches and glass vials, leather vambraces, brown boots; all fabric opaque, nothing see-through. Keep her face exactly as beautiful as in the sheet: young, smooth clear skin, light brown-green eyes. NO bow, NO quiver, NO arrows, NO dagger, NO cheetah, nothing in her hands. Plain flat light-grey background, soft even studio light, no cast shadow, no floor texture. NO text, NO labels, NO frame, NO second view. Real skin and real fabric, like a still from a high-end live-action fantasy film. NOT anime, NOT illustration, NOT painting.
